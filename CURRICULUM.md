# Curriculum basis and proposed progression

Reviewed October 27, 2026. This is a focused supplementary practice app, not a complete grades 3–8 curriculum or an official Wilton product.

## Verified public context

[Wilton Public Schools Mathematics Home](https://www.wiltonps.org/154956_3) states that K–12 math aligns to Connecticut Common Core standards. It identifies Illustrative Math as the problem-based resource in K–8 and Algebra I in grade 8. The overview emphasizes conceptual understanding, fluency, representations, reasoning, and teacher guidance. It links grade/course outlines, but this release does not claim a verified current unit sequence, teacher assignment list, or complete review of those outlines.

The public [Common Core mathematics standards](https://corestandards.org/mathematics-standards/) supply the content references below. The existence of a standards connection is not proof that these short activities cover the whole standard.

## Longer map: instructional proposal

These groupings, activity formats, example selection, support fading, and order are our design decisions. They are not verified Wilton pacing requirements. Prerequisites are revisitable and never enforced by a score or locked grade gate.

1. **Number relationships:** place value, making ten, doubles, decomposition, addition/subtraction inverses, number lines. Foundation review for all students.
2. **Whole-number facts and relationships:** equal groups, arrays, multiplication/division, unknown positions, equality, operation properties. Grade 3 content, revisited as needed.
3. **Rational-number bridge:** [3.NF](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/NF/) unit fractions and number-line quantities → [4.NF](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/4/NF/) equivalence and operations → [5.NF](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/5/NF/) fraction operations and [5.NBT.7](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/5/NBT/) decimal operations → [6.NS.1](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/NS/) fraction division. Revisit place value throughout.
4. **Expressions and rules:** [5.OA.1–3](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/5/OA/) grouping/order, interpreting expressions and patterns; [6.EE.2–7, 6.EE.9](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/EE/) quantities represented by variables, operation properties, equivalent expressions, one-step equations, and input/output relationships. Requires the rational-number bridge before claiming grade-level readiness.
5. **Signed and proportional quantities:** [6.NS.5–8](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/NS/) signed magnitude/coordinates; [6.RP](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/RP/) ratios and unit rates; [7.NS](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/NS/) rational operations; [7.RP](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/RP/) proportional relationships; [7.EE.4](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/EE/) two-step equations. Develop these connections together rather than treating fact recall as sufficient preparation.
6. **Linear relationships:** [8.EE.5–8](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/8/EE/) linear equations and slope connections; [8.F.1–5](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/8/F/) tables, graphs, rules, initial values, linear and nonlinear examples. Requires proportional reasoning, signed arithmetic, and equation understanding.

## Teacher pilot notes

- Start with one worked example, one short round, and an explanation. The first groups question includes a model; later prompts invite unsupported attempts, with help still available.
- Connect requires selecting representations; other Practice tasks require producing quantities, including missing numbers and numeric expressions. Explore lets students change an array with keyboard-accessible controls. Paper tasks provide space for drawing. Freehand digital drawing is not included.
- Reflections have no automated correctness score. Ask students to use a model, related fact, or substitution, and follow up on unclear reasoning.
- Repeated rounds reuse a limited bank. Use novel examples and observations over time before making judgments about retention, transfer, or readiness. Neither completion nor one short round means mastery.
- Browser totals belong to the browser profile, not an identified student. This release does not provide teacher dashboards, cross-device records, placement decisions, or student accounts.
- Before classroom use, review the question bank and answer key; try actual student devices, keyboard-only use, screen readers, and local printers; check current teacher pacing; and observe whether the feedback addresses students’ explanations.

## Multiplication studio expansion

All 169 ordered factor pairs from 0–12 are now available in selectable-table flashcards and untimed quizzes. The one-digit subset supports 3.OA.5 and 3.OA.7 practice; factors 10–12 are our optional instructional extension. This expansion does not add a verified Wilton requirement or alter the longer curriculum map. The content includes zero/one, doubles, decompositions using 5 and 10, and connections to division only when both factors are nonzero. Each strategy equation is verified across the complete bank. Flashcards are self-reflection; quizzes separate in-question support from checks without it, and all retries remain separate. Broader grade 3 applications and a classroom fluency assessment still require other work.
